# Hack Day challenges

**Hacktoberfest Hack Day • Hyderabad | MLH × DEV × React Hyderabad**

These are the three planned challenges, based on the final v6 problem statements. Choose a focused project and use the relevant brief to guide your build and demo.

| Track | Challenge | Source brief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Best Open-Source AI Project](challenges/open-source-ai.md) | [PDF](challenges/briefs/open-source-ai.pdf) |
| 2 | [Best Use of Gemma 4](challenges/gemma-4.md) | [PDF](challenges/briefs/gemma-4.pdf) |
| 3 | [Build on elah](challenges/elah.md) | [PDF](challenges/briefs/elah.pdf) |

The first two briefs describe MLH challenges. Build on elah is the planned local challenge based on the supplied technical brief; its examples are optional idea directions within that challenge, and original ideas are welcome. All three final briefs use a two-hour (120-minute) build plan.

Idea examples are inspiration, not additional mandatory requirements. For eligibility, awards, build times, deadlines, and whether a project may enter multiple categories, follow the organizers and the [MLH event page](https://www.mlh.com/events/react-hyderabad-hack-day/challenges). The suggested build plans in the PDFs do not establish the event schedule.

Use [SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md](SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md) to explain the challenge you are entering and show evidence for its requirements.
